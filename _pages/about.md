Im particularly interested in the neuropsycholgical connection of diseases, intenting to pursue a career in neuropsychology, either clinically, or in a research enviroment. Outside of university, i am a disability support worker, world traveller - specifically solo backpacking (32 countries and counting), film photographer, and oil-paint artist.
